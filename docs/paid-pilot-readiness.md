For the intake cohort it reports how many had and had not received an issue by the report end; the latter can include cases that already closed or were handed off, so it is not described as “still awaiting issue.” The agent-enrollment cohort reports aggregate counts for agent-origin proposals, handoffs, agent-origin closure without handoff, staff closure without a recorded handoff, closure after handoff, and cases still with the agent or awaiting staff after handoff. It also reports median and 90th-percentile hours from enrollment to an agent proposal and from enrollment to agent-origin closure.

After an approved pilot has non-simulated agent-managed cases, an operator can produce the aggregates from the production app container with `docker compose -p aeq-prod exec app node node_modules/tsx/dist/cli.mjs scripts/report-case-agent-metrics.ts --from=YYYY-MM-DD --to=YYYY-MM-DD`. Dates are UTC calendar days; `--to` is exclusive. Both cohorts select their opening event in the chosen window and read later lifecycle events before its end date.
