# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Parties to organizational, employment, commercial, housing and consumer disputes use role-appropriate views of the same case workspace. Program staff operate the service, handle exceptions, and oversee the process. For an opted-in program, an autonomous case agent handles configured, eligible routine retail cases from intake through an evidence-grounded proposal without case-manager action. Parties exchange positions, control their own submissions, and give or withhold version-specific consent.

## Product Purpose

Aequitas is a bilingual dispute-resolution service grounded in `Aequitas Core.txt`. It organizes contested narratives, evidence, competing interpretations, context, lived experience and possible repair into an attributable case record. Staff-led cases remain supported. In agent-managed cases, deterministic program rules and evidence checks constrain an autonomous workflow that can invite the respondent, collect evidence and positions, issue a structured non-binding proposal, and close after both parties consent, the complainant withdraws, or the program's explicit rule permits respondent withdrawal. The case agent does not determine truth or law; uncertainty, out-of-scope files, failed checks, declines and requests for a person go to program staff. The agent is off by default until the program, provider, oversight and legal gates are satisfied.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exacting, and accountable. The interface should create confidence through clarity and restraint, not legal or algorithmic overclaiming.

## Anti-references

Avoid automated-justice theatre, opaque AI scoring, dashboard vanity metrics, pre-selected consent, and generic fintech/SaaS decoration.

## Design Principles

1. Consent is explicit, version-bound, and controlled by each party.
2. Proposals cite their evidence and state their limits; the case agent cannot turn them into binding rulings.
3. Every role sees its authority and next action.
4. Compliance is evidenced, not asserted.
5. Simulated data can teach the workflow but must never impersonate live evidence.
6. Experience, evidence, identity and causal interpretation remain distinct.
7. Disagreement and uncertainty are preserved; synthesis never requires forced consensus.
8. Every assessment can be traced and challenged. A person handles escalations and remains available whenever a party asks.

See [the backend contract](docs/aequitas-core-backend.md) for implemented capabilities and current limits.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, clear labels, status announcements, and color-independent state cues.
