# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Parties to organizational, employment, commercial, housing and consumer disputes, together with assigned human case managers, use the same case workspace from role-appropriate views. They need to understand the evidence, exchange positions, give or withhold consent, and review a reasoned recommendation without losing accountability.

## Product Purpose

Aequitas is a conflict knowledge system grounded in `Aequitas Core.txt`. It turns contested narratives into traceable maps of claims, evidence, competing interpretations, context, lived experience and possible repair. Dialectical inquiry tests alternatives, trialectical inquiry examines neglected structural factors, and triangulation evaluates evidentiary pathways. These maps support a documented, human-reviewable dispute-resolution process. Success means a proposed outcome is clearly distinguished from a decision, every material step is attributable, and no party is represented as having consented before it has done so.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exacting, and accountable. The interface should create confidence through clarity and restraint, not legal or algorithmic overclaiming.

## Anti-references

Avoid automated-justice theatre, opaque AI scoring, dashboard vanity metrics, pre-selected consent, and generic fintech/SaaS decoration.

## Design Principles

1. Consent is an explicit, reversible state.
2. Recommendations explain their evidence, limits, and reviewer.
3. Every role sees its authority and next action.
4. Compliance is evidenced, not asserted.
5. Simulated data can teach the workflow but must never impersonate live evidence.
6. Experience, evidence, identity and causal interpretation remain distinct.
7. Disagreement and uncertainty are preserved; synthesis never requires forced consensus.
8. Every assessment can be traced, challenged and revised by a human.

See [the backend contract](docs/aequitas-core-backend.md) for implemented capabilities and current limits.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, clear labels, status announcements, and color-independent state cues.
